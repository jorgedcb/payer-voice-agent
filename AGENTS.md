# Agent instructions

This repository is public. Everything written into it or onto its GitHub pages is
published: code, comments, fixtures, commit messages, and issue and pull request text,
including every earlier version of an edited description. Removing something later
leaves it in that history, so check text before you commit or post it.

Nothing published here may identify a real call or the people and businesses on it: no
internal call or trace IDs, no names of patients, representatives, clinics or providers,
and no real member IDs, NPIs, tax IDs, addresses, phone numbers or call references.
Describe a call by payer and date ("a 2026-09-22 UnitedHealthcare call") and use
synthetic values, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) requires.

Setup, checks and testing are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
